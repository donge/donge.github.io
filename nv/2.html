在 Juniper，我从 MTS4 晋升到 Staff Engineer 和 Manager，负责 SRX 的 PFE（Packet Forwarding Engine）。控制面把路由与下一跳通过 NHDB 下载到 PFE，Parser 解析报文，JEXEC 执行转发指令，随后应用下一跳、QoS、ACL 等处理。我们的软件转发功能还支持 IP/MPLS、MPLS VPN 等特性。我也参与 Broadcom 平台 bring-up、MIPS SDK 适配和 FPGA 驱动；在 SRX1500 上使用 KVM/QEMU、DPDK、SR-IOV、VirtIO 实现控制面与数据面分离以及近线速处理。

At Juniper, I progressed from MTS4 to Staff Engineer and Manager and worked on the SRX PFE—Packet Forwarding Engine. The control plane downloaded routes and next hops to the PFE through the NHDB; the parser classified packets; JEXEC executed forwarding instructions; and the pipeline applied next-hop, QoS and ACL processing. The software forwarding implementation also supported features including IP/MPLS and MPLS VPNs. I also worked on Broadcom platform bring-up, MIPS SDK adaptation and an FPGA driver. On SRX1500, we used KVM/QEMU, DPDK, SR-IOV and VirtIO for control/data-plane separation and near-line-rate processing.
