Fabric 拓扑

Fabric topology

Leaf–Spine、Clos/fat-tree、多级 Clos、ToR/EoR、双归属；radix、跳数、双截带宽、超售、故障域、布线与扩容。

Leaf–spine, Clos/fat-tree, multistage Clos, ToR/EoR and dual-homing. Evaluate radix, hops, bisection bandwidth, oversubscription, failure domains, cabling and expansion.

Leaf 的下联/上联比例决定局部超售，但端到端能力还受上层级和故障后剩余路径约束。检查任一 Spine 下线后容量是否仍满足 SLO，以及机架、供电和光缆故障是否共享同一失败域。

Leaf downlink/uplink ratios determine local oversubscription, but upper tiers and surviving paths constrain end-to-end capacity. Check SLOs after a spine failure and whether racks, power and fibers share failure domains.

Underlay

Underlay

L3 ECMP、地址/路由规划、BGP/IGP、汇总、故障隔离与 drain；重哈希引起重排，策略可能造成路径不对称。

L3 ECMP, addressing/routing plans, BGP/IGP, aggregation, fault isolation and draining. Rehashing can reorder traffic; policy can create asymmetric paths.

Drain 应先让路由或负载均衡不再选择设备，再等待在途流量收敛，最后维护。只关闭端口会触发突发重哈希；在维护前后比较路径数量、丢包窗口和长期流迁移情况。

Drain by withdrawing selection from routing/load balancing, waiting for traffic convergence, then maintaining the device. Simply shutting ports triggers abrupt rehashing. Compare path count, loss windows and long-lived flow movement before and after maintenance.

Overlay [S5/S6]

Overlay [S5/S6]

VXLAN：VTEP/VNI、L2 over UDP/IP、封解装与 MTU；EVPN：MAC/IP 可达性、RT/RD、L2/L3VNI、IRB。基础 EVPN RFC 为 MPLS，VXLAN/IRB 需配套规范。

VXLAN: VTEP/VNI, L2 over UDP/IP, encapsulation and MTU. EVPN: MAC/IP reachability, RT/RD, L2/L3VNI and IRB. The base EVPN RFC is MPLS-based; VXLAN/IRB need companion specifications.

VXLAN 提供数据封装，EVPN 分发控制信息，二者分工不同。排查跨租户/跨网段故障时分别检查 VNI→VRF/bridge 映射、EVPN 路由导入策略、远端 VTEP 可达性和隧道邻接。

VXLAN encapsulates data; EVPN distributes control information. For tenant/subnet failures, separately inspect VNI-to-VRF/bridge mappings, EVPN import policy, remote VTEP reachability and tunnel adjacency.

高级行为

Advanced behavior

MAC mobility、ARP/ND suppression、BUM、head-end replication、multihoming、DF election、split-horizon；验证 VTEP 故障、迁移和 underlay 未就绪。

MAC mobility, ARP/ND suppression, BUM, head-end replication, multihoming, DF election and split-horizon. Test VTEP failures, mobility and unresolved underlay reachability.

多归属环境中 DF 决策影响部分多目的流量的转发，split-horizon 用来阻止回送。MAC mobility 的序列和旧路由撤销必须配合；测试迁移过程中旧位置、学习事件和控制面更新相互覆盖的窗口。

In multihoming, DF decisions affect selected multidestination forwarding and split-horizon prevents reflection. MAC-mobility sequencing must coordinate with stale-route withdrawal. Test interactions among old locations, learning events and control updates during moves.

封装字节预算

Encapsulation budget

VXLAN over IPv4 的常见外层增加 14B Ethernet+20B IPv4+8B UDP+8B VXLAN，不含额外 VLAN 和外层 FCS。内层携带 Ethernet 帧而不是仅 IP 包，因此 underlay 的 L3 MTU 需要容纳外层 IP/UDP/VXLAN 和内层二层报文。应逐字段计算，明确设备 MTU 命令的口径，避免把“增加 50B”机械用于所有场景。

Typical VXLAN over IPv4 adds 14B Ethernet+20B IPv4+8B UDP+8B VXLAN, excluding extra VLAN tags and outer FCS. The inner payload includes Ethernet, not just IP, so underlay L3 MTU must cover outer IP/UDP/VXLAN plus the inner frame. Calculate fields explicitly and verify each device's MTU definition rather than applying a universal 50B rule.

实验：跨 VTEP 黑洞

Lab: inter-VTEP black hole

按四层证据排查：本地 MAC/IP 路由是否导入正确 VRF；VNI 是否映射到目标 bridge/VRF；远端 VTEP 的 underlay 路由和邻居是否可用；硬件 tunnel/next-hop/rewrite 是否已安装。最后比较内层和外层镜像。只确认 BGP EVPN 邻接正常无法排除后三层问题。

Check four evidence layers: correct local route import, VNI-to-bridge/VRF mapping, remote VTEP underlay route/neighbor readiness, and installed hardware tunnel/next-hop/rewrite. Compare inner/outer captures last. An established EVPN session does not validate these dependencies.

容量与失败域

Capacity & failure domains

同时统计租户数、每租户 MAC/IP、VTEP 数、复制列表长度和隧道邻居。BUM 的 head-end replication 会将一份输入扩展为多份发送；控制面路由数量不变也可能使复制带宽先满。模拟 Spine 和 VTEP 分别故障，检查重排、收敛与陈旧复制成员。

Track tenants, MAC/IP entries per tenant, VTEPs, replication-list lengths and tunnel neighbors. Head-end BUM replication multiplies transmissions and can exhaust replication bandwidth without increasing route count. Fail spines and VTEPs separately; inspect reordering, convergence and stale replication members.
