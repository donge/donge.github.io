物理与端口

PHY & ports

SerDes、NRZ/PAM4、PCS/PMA、协商、link training、FEC、BER；lane/logical/front-panel 映射、breakout、admin/oper、热插拔与 flap。

SerDes, NRZ/PAM4, PCS/PMA, negotiation, link training, FEC and BER. Map lanes/logical/front-panel ports; handle breakout, admin/oper state, hot-plug and flaps.

链路 up 前通常经过模块检测、lane 配置、训练和 FEC 对齐。排障时同时看 pre-FEC 错误、不可纠错块和 flap 时间线；大量已纠正错误可能提示链路裕量下降，即使尚未形成业务丢包。

Link establishment involves module detection, lane configuration, training and FEC alignment. Correlate pre-FEC errors, uncorrectable blocks and flap timelines. Rising corrected errors can indicate declining link margin before service-visible loss occurs.

帧与链路预算

Frames & link budget

DA/SA、802.1Q、EtherType、FCS、MTU/Jumbo；线速计入 preamble/SFD 和 IFG。检查 DAC/AOC/光模块、光功率、温度及 FEC 错误。

DA/SA, 802.1Q, EtherType, FCS and MTU/jumbo frames. Line-rate accounting includes preamble/SFD and IFG. Check DAC/AOC/optics, optical power, temperature and FEC errors.

MTU 通常描述三层报文容量，而线上帧还包含二层和封装开销。验证隧道时计算内层帧、VXLAN/UDP/IP 与外层 Ethernet，并确认每跳 MTU；只在端点启用 Jumbo 不能消除中间链路黑洞。

MTU generally describes L3 packet capacity, whereas wire frames include L2 and encapsulation overhead. For tunnels, budget the inner frame, VXLAN/UDP/IP and outer Ethernet, and verify each hop. Endpoint jumbo settings cannot fix an intermediate MTU black hole.

二层交换

L2 switching

MAC 学习/老化/移动、FDB、VLAN/PVID、tagged/untagged、QinQ、未知单播/广播/多播泛洪；表满行为与风暴抑制。

MAC learning/aging/moves, FDB, VLAN/PVID, tagged/untagged ports, QinQ and unknown-unicast/broadcast/multicast flooding. Define full-table behavior and storm control.

源 MAC 学习建立“MAC+广播域→端口”映射；目的未知时可能泛洪，命中后单播。MAC move 会改变出口，老化释放不活跃记录；需测试环路、伪造源地址和快速迁移对学习速率及 CPU 的影响。

Source learning maps MAC plus broadcast domain to a port; unknown destinations may flood, learned destinations unicast. Moves change egress and aging removes inactive entries. Test loops, spoofed sources and rapid mobility against learning rate and CPU load.

环路与聚合

Loops & aggregation

STP/RSTP/MSTP、BPDU guard、LAG/LACP、哈希偏斜、MLAG split-brain/peer-link；用 LLDP 与镜像定位。Link up 不等于业务可达。

STP/RSTP/MSTP, BPDU guard, LAG/LACP, hash skew and MLAG split-brain/peer-link failures. Use LLDP and mirroring. Link up does not prove service reachability.

LACP 协商成员资格，但实际流量通常按 hash 分配，单流一般不会自动占满全部成员带宽。MLAG 要明确 peer-link 中断与双活判定：错误地同时保持两侧活动可能引发重复流量或环路。

LACP negotiates membership, while hashing usually distributes traffic; one flow generally cannot consume the entire bundle. For MLAG, define peer-link failure and dual-active detection. Incorrect simultaneous activity can create duplication or loops.

报文逐跳解释

Packet-by-packet explanation

入端口先决定允许的帧与 VLAN 归属，未打标签帧通常根据 PVID 分类。交换机以源 MAC 更新学习状态，再按目的 MAC 和广播域查询出口；出口规则决定标签保留或移除。抓到同一 MAC 不足以证明处于同一转发域，必须同时匹配 VLAN、bridge 和端口模式。

Ingress rules determine permitted frames and VLAN membership; untagged frames are commonly classified by PVID. Source MAC updates learning state, destination MAC plus broadcast domain determines egress, and egress rules retain/remove tags. Matching MAC alone does not imply the same forwarding domain: check VLAN, bridge and port mode.

实验：单向不通

Lab: one-way connectivity

设 A 接 access VLAN 10，B 接 trunk 但缺少 VLAN 10。先观察 A 发帧时入口计数增加、FDB 是否学到 A，再检查 B 出口 discard 与 VLAN 成员。反向报文可能被分入另一 PVID，表现不同。修正成员后重新比较双向已知单播和未知单播，避免被已有 FDB 或泛洪掩盖。

A uses access VLAN 10 while B's trunk omits VLAN 10. Check A ingress counters and source learning, then B egress discards and membership. Reverse traffic may enter a different PVID. After correcting membership, compare known and unknown unicast in both directions so FDB state/flooding does not hide the issue.

链路与逻辑隔离诊断

Separating physical & logical faults

CRC/不可纠错 FEC 指向链路质量，VLAN discard 指向逻辑规则，pause 指向流控，两者可同时存在。采用同端口换模块、同模块换端口、降低速率等受控实验定位因素；每次只改一项并记录版本、FEC 模式和误码时间序列。

CRC/uncorrectable FEC suggests link quality, VLAN discards suggest logical rules, and pause suggests flow control; multiple causes can coexist. Controlled module/port swaps or speed changes isolate factors. Change one variable at a time and record versions, FEC mode and error timelines.
