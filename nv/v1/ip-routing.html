转发基础

Forwarding basics

IPv4/IPv6、TTL/Hop Limit、LPM、下一跳/rewrite、ICMP；ARP/ND/DAD、邻居容量、PMTUD/黑洞、IPv6 扩展头与不由路由器分片。

IPv4/IPv6, TTL/Hop Limit, LPM, next hops/rewrites and ICMP. ARP/ND/DAD, neighbor scale, PMTUD/black holes, IPv6 extension headers and no IPv6 router fragmentation.

转发查找与邻接解析是两个阶段：路由确定下一跳，邻接提供二层 rewrite。邻居未解析时可能暂存、丢弃或上送，具体依实现。检查 PMTUD 时保留必要 ICMP/ICMPv6，避免 ACL 把“包太大”反馈过滤掉。

Route lookup and adjacency resolution are separate: routing selects a next hop, adjacency supplies L2 rewrite. Unresolved neighbors may queue, drop or trap traffic depending on implementation. Preserve required ICMP/ICMPv6 so ACLs do not block packet-too-big feedback.

状态链

State chain

协议选路 → RIB → FIB → 下一跳组 → ASIC；递归解析、邻接未就绪、撤销顺序、批量编程、黑洞/环路窗口。

Protocol selection → RIB → FIB → next-hop group → ASIC. Handle recursion, unresolved adjacency, withdrawal ordering, bulk programming and black-hole/loop windows.

RIB 中最佳路径不一定已进入 FIB，FIB 中条目也未必已下到 ASIC。排障应关联前缀、VRF、下一跳/OID 和编程错误；更新大量前缀时测量应用完成进度，而不只记录协议收敛时间。

A best RIB path may not yet reach the FIB, and a FIB entry may not yet reach the ASIC. Correlate prefix, VRF, next-hop/OID and programming errors. During large updates, measure application completion, not just protocol convergence.

控制协议

Control protocols

BGP eBGP/iBGP、属性/策略、RR、MP-BGP、GR、max-prefix；OSPF/IS-IS、LSDB/SPF；BFD 定时器与硬件卸载，关注 route churn 和背压。

BGP eBGP/iBGP, attributes/policy, RR, MP-BGP, GR and max-prefix; OSPF/IS-IS, LSDB/SPF; BFD timers and hardware offload. Watch route churn and backpressure.

BGP 策略决定哪些路径进入候选集，BFD 只负责快速活性检测，不计算路由。缩短 BFD 定时器会增加处理压力和误报风险；GR 可保留旧转发状态，但旧下一跳已不可达时仍可能形成黑洞。

BGP policy controls candidate paths; BFD detects liveness rather than computing routes. Short BFD timers increase processing demand and false-positive risk. GR may retain forwarding state, but an unreachable stale next hop can still black-hole traffic.

转发分支

Forwarding features

VRF、PBR、ECMP/UCMP、resilient hashing、uRPF、内层 hash 熵；IGMP/MLD、PIM、多播复制按场景深入。

VRF, PBR, ECMP/UCMP, resilient hashing, uRPF and inner-header hash entropy. Explore IGMP/MLD, PIM and multicast replication as required by the deployment.

ECMP 对多个下一跳做分配；resilient hashing 的目标是成员变化时减少无关流迁移。uRPF 的严格模式依赖回程路径，非对称网络中需谨慎。多播还需分别考虑组成员状态、复制树和出口资源。

ECMP distributes traffic among next hops; resilient hashing minimizes unrelated flow movement after membership changes. Strict uRPF depends on reverse-path selection and needs care with asymmetry. Multicast separately needs membership state, replication trees and egress resources.

路由对象依赖链

Route dependency chain

以 VRF blue 中 10.2.0.0/24 为例：选路得到下一跳 192.0.2.2，递归解析其出口接口，再通过邻居记录得到目的 MAC，构造 rewrite，加入下一跳组，最终安装前缀引用。任一依赖失败都可能导致“协议路由存在但不能转发”，所以诊断输出应展示依赖就绪状态。

For 10.2.0.0/24 in VRF blue, selection yields next hop 192.0.2.2, recursion resolves the interface, neighbor state supplies destination MAC, rewrite is built, the group is populated and the prefix references it. Any missing dependency can leave a protocol route without forwarding; diagnostics should expose dependency readiness.

时序：下一跳失效

Timeline: next-hop failure

T0 链路故障；T1 BFD/链路事件检测；T2 协议或编排撤销成员；T3 ASIC 确认；T4 流量稳定。分别测 T1−T0、T3−T1 和 T4−T3。若协议已收敛但 ASIC 更新队列积压，缩短 BFD 定时器不能解决主要瓶颈，反而可能增大事件压力。

T0 link fails; T1 detection; T2 protocol/orchestration withdraws member; T3 ASIC confirms; T4 traffic stabilizes. Measure each interval. If protocols converge but hardware updates queue up, shorter BFD timers do not address the bottleneck and can increase event pressure.

边界与判定

Boundaries & verdict

覆盖默认路由、等长前缀、递归下一跳、邻居老化、IPv6 PMTUD、多 VRF 相同地址与 ECMP 成员变化。验证不只看 ping：固定五元组观察路径与 TTL/rewrite，检查 ICMP 错误、ASIC route/neighbor 和异常报文 policer。

Cover default/equal-length prefixes, recursion, neighbor aging, IPv6 PMTUD, overlapping VRF addresses and ECMP changes. Beyond ping, inspect a fixed flow's path and TTL/rewrite, ICMP errors, ASIC route/neighbor entries and exception policers.
