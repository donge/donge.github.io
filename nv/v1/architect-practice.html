架构师方法学与影响力

Architect methodology & influence

前 26 页是技术；这一页补上 JD 明确要求、但技术速查表通常缺失的架构师软能力：方法学、权衡、领导力、开源标准与影响力。

The previous 26 pages are technical; this page adds the architect's non-technical requirements the JD explicitly asks for but a technical cheatsheet usually omits: methodology, tradeoffs, leadership, open-source/standards and influence.

架构方法学

Architecture methodology

从需求到落地：需求 → 约束 → 架构决策 → 接口契约 → 验证。用三平面（管理/控制/数据）、职责与状态所有者建模；区分期望态、软件已应用态、硬件实际态，用 generation 与 reconcile 处理重复、乱序与漂移。

From requirements to delivery: requirements → constraints → architecture decisions → interface contracts → validation. Model with three planes (management/control/data), responsibilities and state owners; distinguish desired, software-applied and actual-hardware state, using generations and reconcile for duplicates, reordering and drift.

每个功能用统一检查表：输入、状态所有者、执行路径、共享资源、失败报告、结果验证、重启恢复。用可观测证据代替组件名，避免“听起来懂、经不起追问”。

Apply one checklist per feature: inputs, state ownership, execution path, shared resources, failure reporting, result verification, restart recovery. Use observable evidence instead of component names to avoid sounding informed without substance.

权衡与决策记录

Tradeoffs & decision records

常见权衡：可移植性 / 芯片能力、吞吐 / 延迟、隔离 / 利用率、内核 / 用户态、同步 / 异步、通用 / 专用。用记分卡量化约束（容量、时延、恢复上界、成本），用实测而非口号决策。

Common tradeoffs: portability / chip capability, throughput / latency, isolation / utilization, kernel / user space, sync / async, general / purpose-built. Quantify constraints on a scorecard (capacity, latency, recovery bound, cost) and decide from measurement, not slogans.

架构决策记录（ADR）写清背景、备选、选择与后果；实验一次只改一个变量以便归因，并记录可复现的基准。

An Architecture Decision Record (ADR) captures context, options, choice and consequences. Change one variable at a time for attribution, and record reproducible benchmarks.

领导力与跨团队

Leadership & cross-team

把需求映射为模块、接口与平台能力，跨协议/平台/转发/硬件/测试协作；用清晰接口 + 书面规格 + 评审门，与 Chip/FW/HW/PM 建立共识。跨地域团队靠书面契约与异步评审，用里程碑与健康门槛而非口头同步。

Map requirements to modules, interfaces and platform capabilities; collaborate across protocol/platform/forwarding/hardware/test. Use clear interfaces + written specs + review gates, building consensus with Chip/FW/HW/PM. Distributed teams rely on written contracts and async review, using milestones and health gates rather than verbal sync.

故障时先保留现场（寄存器、计数器、版本快照）再行动，避免恢复动作抹掉根因证据。

On failure, preserve evidence (registers, counters, version snapshots) before acting, so recovery does not erase the root cause.

开源与标准

Open source & standards

相关社区与标准：SAI/OCP、SONiC、UEC（Ultra Ethernet）、IEEE/IETF。架构师代表公司在开源与 SDO 发声，把内部需求变成提案与补丁，用共识影响路线。

Relevant communities and standards: SAI/OCP, SONiC, UEC (Ultra Ethernet), IEEE/IETF. Architects represent the company in open source and SDOs, turning internal requirements into proposals and patches and influencing roadmaps through consensus.

把标准状态（草稿/稳定/废弃）纳入技术选型；AI 网络方向尤其关注 UEC 与无损以太网的演进。

Fold standards status (draft/stable/deprecated) into technology choices; for AI networking, watch the evolution of UEC and lossless Ethernet closely.

专利、白皮书与传播

Patents, whitepapers & communication

把创新写成专利、白皮书、博客与会议分享，用数据讲清价值；招聘方常问「你推动了什么共识」。技术写作与可 demo 的原型是最强的影响力证据。

Turn innovation into patents, whitepapers, blogs and conference talks, communicating value with data; panels often ask "what consensus did you drive?". Technical writing and demoable prototypes are the strongest evidence of influence.

记录设计取舍与基准，便于复用与复盘；对外表达遵循「先结论、后论证、给证据」。

Record design tradeoffs and benchmarks for reuse and retrospectives; when presenting, lead with the conclusion, then the argument, then the evidence.
