线速与排队

Packet rate & queues

pps≈R/[8(L+20)]：R 为 bit/s，L 为含 FCS 帧长，20 为前导/SFD+IFG；100Gb/s、64B≈148.81Mpps。t_queue≈8Q/R，1MB@100Gb/s≈80µs。

pps≈R/[8(L+20)]: R in bit/s, L is frame bytes including FCS, 20 accounts for preamble/SFD+IFG. 100Gb/s at 64B≈148.81Mpps. t_queue≈8Q/R; 1MB at 100Gb/s≈80µs.

R 为以太网 MAC 速率，不要把光线路码/FEC 速率直接代入。64B 包率公式包含线上额外 20B 时间；排队 Q 的测量单位需确认是实际字节还是 cell。公式给量级，实际仍需加调度与流水线成本。

Use Ethernet MAC rate R, not raw optical coded/FEC rate. The 64B packet-rate calculation includes 20B of wire-time overhead. Confirm whether queue occupancy is bytes or cells. Formulas estimate scale; scheduling and pipeline costs remain.

BDP / Headroom

BDP / headroom

BDP=R×RTT/8；400Gb/s×10µs→500,000B。H≥R×T_stop/8+在途/帧/实现余量；T_stop 含检测、生成、传播与响应，BDP 不可直接作 headroom。

BDP=R×RTT/8; 400Gb/s×10µs→500,000B. H≥R×T_stop/8 plus in-flight/frame/implementation margin. T_stop includes detection, generation, propagation and response; BDP is not a direct headroom setting.

Headroom 是暂停反馈生效前吸收在途数据的空间，BDP 描述链路在一个 RTT 内可承载的数据量，二者时间定义不同。核对端口速率、线缆长度、最大帧和多个优先级并发，不使用单一经验值复制全网。

Headroom absorbs in-flight data until pause feedback takes effect; BDP describes capacity over one RTT. Their time definitions differ. Account for port rate, cable length, maximum frames and concurrent priorities rather than copying one empirical value fabric-wide.

拓扑与恢复

Topology & recovery

超售≈下联总带宽/上联总带宽；恢复时间分解为检测、控制处理、硬件编程和流量恢复，并识别可并行阶段。

Oversubscription≈aggregate downlink/uplink bandwidth. Break recovery into detection, control processing, hardware programming and traffic restoration, accounting for overlapping stages.

正常拓扑超售 1:1 不代表故障后仍 1:1。按最大允许失败集合重算上联容量；恢复阶段有时重叠，应从事件时间线测关键路径，而不是机械相加所有计时器。

A 1:1 topology may cease to be 1:1 after failures. Recalculate uplink capacity for allowed failure sets. Recovery stages may overlap; derive the critical path from event timelines instead of blindly adding every timer.

Ring AllReduce

Ring AllReduce

T≈2(N−1)α+2(N−1)M/(N·B)：M 为每 rank 字节数，B 为有效 B/s，α 为每步延迟；未计计算、重叠和竞争。区分 bit/byte、MB/MiB 与有效负载。

T≈2(N−1)α+2(N−1)M/(N·B): M is bytes per rank, B is effective B/s, α is per-step latency. Excludes compute, overlap and contention. Distinguish bits/bytes, MB/MiB and payload rates.

Ring 模型的传输项随 N 增大趋近 2M/B，但延迟项随步数增长，因此小消息未必适合 Ring。B 应取有效瓶颈带宽；节点内/间分层、重叠和规约计算会使实际结果偏离简化式。

The ring transfer term approaches 2M/B as N grows, while the latency term grows with steps, making ring less attractive for some small messages. Use effective bottleneck bandwidth B; hierarchy, overlap and reduction compute cause deviations from the simple model.

单位推导

Deriving the units

R(bit/s)÷8 得 B/s，时间乘 B/s 才是缓冲字节。64B 帧含 FCS，加前导/SFD 8B 与 IFG 12B 得 84B 线上时间，100×10⁹/(84×8)=148.8095×10⁶pps。若工具报 payload throughput，不能直接与该线速比较；需加入 Ethernet、IP、传输和隧道开销。

R(bit/s)/8 gives B/s, and multiplying by time gives buffer bytes. A 64B frame including FCS plus 8B preamble/SFD and 12B IFG occupies 84B wire time: 100×10⁹/(84×8)=148.8095Mpps. Payload throughput needs Ethernet/IP/transport/tunnel overhead before comparison with line rate.

AllReduce 算例

AllReduce worked example

N=8、每 rank M=1GiB、有效 B=25GB/s、α=2µs。传输项 2×7/8×1,073,741,824/25×10⁹≈75.16ms，延迟项 14×2µs=28µs，总计约 75.19ms，尚未计规约计算和竞争。此例大消息传输主导；把 M 降至 1KiB 后，启动延迟将占主导，不能沿用大消息带宽结论。

For N=8, M=1GiB/rank, effective B=25GB/s and α=2µs, transfer≈75.16ms and latency=28µs, total≈75.19ms before reduction compute/contention. Large-message transfer dominates here; at M=1KiB, startup latency dominates, so large-message bandwidth conclusions no longer apply.

误差与使用范围

Error & applicability

这些模型用于估计量级和检查测量是否合理，不能产生通用阈值。需要声明 GB/GiB、单向/双向、有效带宽还是端口速率，以及是否包含计算、重叠和排队。若实测比理想值快，先检查消息口径与计时；比理想值慢则逐项定位损失，而非立即断言网络故障。

Use models for scale and sanity checks, not universal thresholds. State decimal/binary units, directionality, effective versus port bandwidth, and inclusion of compute/overlap/queueing. Faster-than-ideal results suggest accounting/timing issues; slower results require attribution rather than immediate network blame.
