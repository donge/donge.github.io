SRAM / TCAM / LPM

SRAM / TCAM / LPM

Exact match 常用 hash/SRAM，ternary 规则需要掩码匹配，LPM 可由多种专用或组合结构实现。

Exact match often uses hash/SRAM; ternary rules require masks, and LPM may use dedicated or composite structures.

IPv6 key 更宽，范围匹配可能展开，多 stage 复制也消耗额外表项；逻辑规则数不等于物理占用。

IPv6 keys are wider, ranges may expand, and stage replication consumes entries. Logical rule count is not physical occupancy.

Buffer cell 算账

Buffer-cell accounting

若 cell 大小 C、包需缓存 L 字节，简化成本是 ceil(L/C)×C，另加实现 metadata。

With cell size C and buffered length L, simplified cost is ceil(L/C)×C plus implementation metadata.

示例 C=208B、L=1500B，占 8 cells=1664B，约 10.9% 内部余量。208B 只是算例，不代表某款 Spectrum 的规格。

For illustrative C=208B and L=1500B, eight cells consume 1664B, about 10.9% internal overhead. 208B is an example, not a Spectrum specification.

ECMP 资源

ECMP resources

资源至少包含组对象、成员对象、hash buckets 和 next-hop/rewrite。

Resources include groups, members, hash buckets and next-hop/rewrites.

N 个前缀共用 K 路径时不一定消耗 N×K 个成员；是否共享由 NOS/SDK 实现决定。Resilient hash 通过 bucket 间接映射减少成员变化时无关流迁移。

N prefixes sharing K paths need not consume N×K members; sharing depends on NOS/SDK implementation. Resilient hashing uses bucket indirection to reduce unrelated flow movement after membership changes.

PPS 与内部遍历

PPS & internal traversals

回流、镜像、多播会放大内部工作量。

Recirculation, mirroring and multicast amplify internal work.

若资源每秒处理 P 次遍历，比例 f 的包多走一次，简化外部上限 P/(1+f)。此外字节带宽、复制引擎、出口与 CPU trap 都可能先成为瓶颈，Tb/s 不能单独描述处理能力。

For P traversals/s and fraction f requiring an extra pass, a simplified external bound is P/(1+f). Byte bandwidth, replication, egress or CPU traps can bind earlier; Tb/s alone is insufficient.

算例：更新速率与积压

Example: update rate & backlog

假设输入持续 30k 次/s，硬件确认仅 20k 次/s，未合并队列每秒增加 10k；每项含元数据占 256B，约增长 2.56MB/s，60 秒即约 154MB。加大队列只推迟故障。可按同一对象合并到最后目标态、限制入口速率或增加设备编程能力；合并不能跨越必须保留的依赖与屏障。

With 30k updates/s arriving and 20k/s confirmed, an uncoalesced queue grows by 10k/s. At 256B per item including metadata, that is about 2.56MB/s or 154MB in 60s. Larger queues only defer failure. Coalesce by object, limit admission or increase programming capacity, while preserving dependencies and required barriers.
