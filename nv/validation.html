流量字段

Traffic fields

用固定 VLAN、MAC、IP、L4 port 和 sequence payload 建立可识别流。

Build identifiable flows with VLAN/MAC/IP/L4 ports and sequence payloads.

修改 hash 字段扫描 ECMP 分布；区分生成器 offered load、DUT 接收量和接收端序列缺口。捕获未包含 FCS 很常见，不能据此判断线上缺 FCS。

Vary hash fields for ECMP distribution; distinguish offered load, DUT ingress and receiver sequence gaps. Captures often omit FCS without implying absent wire FCS.

延迟测量

Latency measurement

单向时延需要可比较时钟或同一测量域；RTT/2 假设对称，不适合直接推导非对称路径单向尾延迟。

One-way latency needs comparable clocks or one measurement domain.

硬件时间戳与软件时间戳包含的排队范围不同，必须定义取样点。

RTT/2 assumes symmetry and cannot directly infer asymmetric tail latency. Hardware/software timestamps cover different queueing regions; define points precisely.

ECMP 分布统计

ECMP distribution statistics

少量 elephant flow 即使 hash 均匀也可能产生端口偏斜。

A few elephant flows can skew ports despite uniform hashing.

扩大五元组样本并同时统计每路径包数/字节；包数均衡不代表带宽均衡。成员删除后统计迁移比例和乱序，而不只看最终总吞吐。

Expand five-tuple samples and measure packets/bytes per path: packet balance is not bandwidth balance. After member removal measure migration/reordering, not just final throughput.

RDMA / NCCL 测量

RDMA / NCCL measurements

RDMA perftest 与 nccl-tests 覆盖不同层：前者测试 verbs/端点传输，后者还受 GPU、collective 算法与 rank placement 影响。

RDMA perftest probes verbs/endpoint transport; nccl-tests additionally includes GPUs, collective algorithms and placement.

比较结果时固定消息长度、队列深度、QP/rank 数和设备选择。

Fix message size, queue depth, QP/rank count and device selection when comparing.

基于不变量的测试

Invariant-based testing

建立“每个路由引用有效下一跳、总资源有界、重复请求幂等、重启最终收敛、失败不破坏无关业务”等不变量。用随机操作序列生成 create/set/delete/reset，同时保留 seed 和事件日志。Oracle 同时观察期望模型、控制态和真实报文，避免测试只验证 mock 是否按预期被调用。

Define invariants: valid next-hop references, bounded resources, idempotency, restart convergence and unrelated-service preservation. Generate create/set/delete/reset sequences with saved seeds/logs. Compare a reference model, control state and real packets rather than merely checking mock calls.
