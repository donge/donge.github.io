DS field 与 ECN

DS field & ECN

IPv4 DS field 的高 6bit 是 DSCP，低 2bit 是 ECN：00 Not-ECT、10 ECT(0)、01 ECT(1)、11 CE。

The IPv4 DS field contains DSCP in the upper 6 bits and ECN in the lower 2: 00 Not-ECT, 10 ECT(0), 01 ECT(1), 11 CE.

ECN 标记不能随意把 Not-ECT 流量改成支持 ECN；经典拥塞处理仍可能丢包。IPv6 对应 Traffic Class。

Marking cannot arbitrarily turn Not-ECT traffic into ECN-capable traffic; classic handling may drop it. IPv6 uses Traffic Class.

动态阈值 α

Dynamic threshold α

简化模型 T=α×free_shared；α 固定时阈值随剩余空间线性变化。

In T=α×free_shared, fixed α gives a linear relation to remaining space.

SAI dynamic threshold 参数可用指数编码 α=2^n，指数编码与“阈值随拥塞指数下降”不是同一概念。ECN/WRED 阈值与 buffer admission 还可能属于不同资源。

SAI dynamic thresholds can encode α as 2^n; exponent encoding does not mean exponential threshold decay under congestion. ECN/WRED and admission thresholds may reference different resources.

PFC 帧与时间

PFC frame & time

PFC 使用 MAC Control EtherType 0x8808、opcode 0x0101，含优先级使能位和 8 个 pause time。

PFC uses MAC Control EtherType 0x8808, opcode 0x0101, an enable vector and eight pause times.

时间单位是 512 bit-times；100Gb/s 时每 quantum=5.12ns。PFC 暂停的是链路优先级，具体 queue/PG 由映射决定。

Units are 512 bit-times: 5.12ns/quantum at 100Gb/s. It pauses link priorities; queue/PG mapping is configured.

SP / DWRR / Shaping

SP / DWRR / shaping

SP 优先服务高优先级；DWRR 使用 deficit 与 quantum 按字节预算轮转，适应不同包长。

SP serves higher priorities first; DWRR uses deficits/quanta for byte-budgeted rounds across packet sizes.

Shaper 延后发送以控制速率；policer 根据 token 判断颜色或丢弃，不等同排队限速。调度器、队列与端口级层次依 ASIC。

Shapers delay transmission; policers classify/drop by tokens rather than queue for pacing. Queue/port scheduling hierarchy is ASIC-dependent.

队列增长模型

Queue growth model

对某出口，短时间内 ΔQ≈(Σarrival−service)×Δt；速率单位应统一为 B/s。四个 100Gb/s 发送者同时流向一个 100Gb/s 出口，持续 10µs，理想积压约 (400−100)Gb/s×10µs/8=375kB，尚未计其他突发与实现开销。平均利用率低也可能反复出现这种瞬时过载。

For one egress, ΔQ≈(Σarrival−service)×Δt, using B/s. Four 100Gb/s senders targeting one 100Gb/s egress for 10µs ideally build about 375kB, before other bursts/overheads. Low average utilization can coexist with repeated instantaneous overload.
